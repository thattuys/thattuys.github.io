Daevanion Board Solver

Paint the board as it looks in game. The cheapest route to every node you are after appears as you go.

Layout text

One line per row. A dot is empty, S is the start, 1 to 4 is a node of that cost. A wanted grey is w. An ignored green, blue or orange is g, b or o.

Boards are kept in this browser, so copy this text to back one up or share it. A smaller layout, such as an 11 by 11 board, is centred on its start.

Paint

Drag to paint, right-drag to erase, Ctrl+Z to undo. Each brush shows its key. For nodes the key is also the point cost.

Mark

Wanted: a grey the route must include. Ignored: a coloured node you do not need, bought only when that is cheaper than going round it. Click a marked node again to take the mark off.

Route

Ringed nodes are the ones to buy. Faded nodes are skipped.

Board totals

Check these against the game to catch a slip while painting.
